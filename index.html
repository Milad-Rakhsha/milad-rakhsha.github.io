About

I am a senior simulation engineer at NVIDIA, where I work on Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, and Newton. I develop physics solvers and simulation tools for robotics, with a focus on reinforcement learning and robot learning applications.

I joined NVIDIA’s Isaac Gym team in 2022. My work spans the simulation stack, from developing low-level physics solvers to building robotics applications in Isaac Sim and training robots with reinforcement learning in Isaac Lab. I bring experience in both the numerical methods behind a simulator and the practical challenges of using it for robot learning and development.

Before joining NVIDIA, I worked as a simulation engineer at Roblox from 2020 to 2022, where I developed an efficient aerodynamic model for real-time game simulation. Before Roblox, I worked as a research scientist at Walt Disney Animation Studios on frictional contact solvers.

During my PhD at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, I contributed to Project Chrono and was one of the early developers of its fluid solver. My doctoral work helped establish the fluid–solid interaction capabilities in Chrono::FSI.
