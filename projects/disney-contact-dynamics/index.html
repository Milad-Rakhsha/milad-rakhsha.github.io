Frictional contact dynamics at Disney Animation

Walt Disney Animation Studios · Research Scientist · Before joining Roblox in 2020

Before joining Roblox, I worked as a research scientist at Walt Disney Animation Studios. My research focused on an accurate, second-order solver for frictional contact, using a nonsmooth generalized-α method and an interior-point method (IPM).

I was working toward a paper on this research, but it was never published. In 2020, I received a full-time offer from Roblox that I was excited to take, and left Disney to join its simulation team.
