Isaac Lab: A GPU-Accelerated Simulation Framework for Multi-Modal Robot Learning

2025

Isaac Lab integrates GPU-parallel physics, photorealistic rendering, actuator and sensor models, and training infrastructure for reinforcement and imitation learning. The framework supports batched environments, domain randomization, and data collection for locomotion, manipulation, and learning from demonstrations.

Lagrangian vs. Eulerian: An Analysis of Two Solution Methods for Free-Surface Flows and Fluid Solid Interaction Problems

Fluids 2021 Best Paper Award (MDPI)

Fluids 6(12), 460 · 2021

We compare SPH and an Eulerian finite-element formulation on free-surface and fluid–solid interaction benchmarks. The study examines solution accuracy, interface evolution, and computational cost, including cases with moving rigid bodies and deformable structures.

Paper · Research notes

Modeling Granular Material Dynamics and Its Two-Way Coupling with Moving Solid Bodies Using a Continuum Representation and the SPH Method

Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering 385, 114022 · 2021

We use a continuum constitutive description and SPH discretization to simulate granular flow with two-way coupling to moving solids. The GPU implementation addresses large deformations and uses particle shifting to maintain a regular particle distribution.
